Nanchang Orient Special Abrasives Factory ("we", "us", or "our") operates this website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our site or submit an inquiry through our contact form.

南昌市东方特种磨具厂（以下简称「我们」）运营本网站。本隐私政策说明您访问网站或通过联系表单提交询盘时，我们如何收集、使用和保护相关信息。

Information We Collect我们收集的信息

When you use our contact or inquiry form, we may collect: your name, company name, email address, phone number (if provided), country/region, product interest, and message content. We also receive standard technical data such as browser type and approximate access time through our hosting provider's server logs.

当您使用联系/询盘表单时，我们可能收集：姓名、公司名称、电子邮箱、电话号码（如提供）、国家/地区、产品意向及留言内容。此外，托管服务商的服务器日志可能记录浏览器类型、访问时间等常规技术信息。

How We Use Information信息的使用方式

  • To respond to your inquiries, quotes, and partnership requests
  • To communicate about products, orders, or custom specifications you request
  • To improve our website and customer service
  • To comply with applicable laws when required
  • 回复您的询盘、报价及合作咨询
  • 就您所咨询的产品、订单或定制规格与您沟通
  • 改进网站与客户服务
  • 在法律法规要求时履行合规义务

Sharing & Storage共享与存储

We do not sell your personal information. Inquiry data is transmitted by email to our business mailbox and may be processed by our hosting and email service providers (e.g. Vercel, SMTP provider) solely to deliver and operate the service. We retain inquiry records only as long as needed for business correspondence and legal compliance.
