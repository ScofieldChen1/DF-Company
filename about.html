Who We Are我们是谁

Orient Abrasives东方磨具

Est. 1986 · 40 Years of Manufacturing · Northern Nanchang, Jiangxi

创立于 1986 年 · 四十载专业制造 · 江西南昌北郊

Nanchang Orient Special Rubbing Factory is situated in the northern suburbs of Nanchang, adjacent to the Chang-Jiu Expressway — ensuring fast, reliable logistics for domestic and export orders. Since our founding in 1986, we have spent four decades specializing in special-shaped grinding wheels, mounted points, and custom abrasive tools. We are a dedicated manufacturer with full in-house production — not a trading company or intermediary.

南昌市东方特种磨具厂坐落于南昌市北郊、昌九高速公路沿线，交通十分便利，便于国内外订单发运。自 1986 年创立以来，我们专注异型砂轮、带杆磨头等特种磨具的研发与生产，历经四十载积累 — 自主制造，非贸易中间商。

Drawing on advanced processes and equipment from Japan, the United States, and other leading markets, we continuously expand our product range. Our abrasives are trusted by customers worldwide. Core lines include special-shaped wheels, mounted grinding points, grinding sleeves, active-shank flap wheels, sponge wheels, wool wheels, bowl brushes, pen brushes, pen-type tools, and custom non-standard specifications — all available for OEM and bulk order.

近年来，本厂持续引进日本、美国等发达国家的先进工艺与设备，不断研制开发新产品，产品深受国内外用户好评。主营品类涵盖：异型砂轮、配套带杆磨头、砂圈、活动带杆页轮、海绵轮、羊毛轮、碗刷、笔刷、笔型磨具等，支持各类非标规格定制，欢迎国内外用户订购。

Three Core Application Fields三大核心应用领域

  • Shipbuilding造船工业 — Hull weld cleanup, steel surface preparation, and marine-grade metal finishing for shipyards worldwide. — 船体焊缝清理、钢材表面处理、船级金属抛光，服务全球造船基地。
  • Aerospace航天航空 — Precision grinding of alloy components, turbine blades, and aerospace-grade surface finishing where tolerance is critical. — 合金构件、涡轮叶片精密磨削及航空级表面处理，满足航天级精度要求。
  • Automotive汽车制造 — Polishing and grinding solutions for body panels, engine parts, and OEM production lines across the supply chain. — 车身面板、发动机部件抛光打磨及汽车 OEM 产线配套，服务主流车企供应链。
