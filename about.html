Company Profile企业概况

Orient Abrasives东方磨具

Est. 1986 · Four Decades · Special Abrasives Factory · Northern Nanchang

1986 年创立 · 四十年老店 · 特种磨具厂 · 江西南昌北郊

Nanchang Orient Special Abrasives Factory was founded in 1986 in the core of the Chang-Jiu industrial corridor, with direct access to northern Nanchang's transport hub. Over four decades of dedicated development, we have evolved from an early industry pioneer into a specialized abrasives enterprise focused on precision manufacturing and custom non-standard production. As a source manufacturer with nearly forty years of industry heritage, we remain committed to delivering the "final millimeter" solutions for precision industrial processing across China and worldwide.

南昌市东方特种磨具厂始创于 1986 年，扎根昌九工业走廊核心区位，坐拥南昌北郊交通枢纽，四十载深耕不辍，已从改革开放初期的行业拓荒者，成长为集自主研发、精密制造、非标定制于一体的特种磨具专业企业。作为拥有近四十年行业积淀的源头制造厂商，我们始终致力于为中国乃至全球工业精密加工提供"最后一毫米"的解决方案。

1986 Founding创立

Factory Founded in Northern Nanchang南昌北郊建厂

Nanchang Orient Special Abrasives Factory established beside the Chang-Jiu Expressway — specializing in special-shaped grinding wheels and mounted points from day one.

南昌市东方特种磨具厂成立，扎根昌九高速公路旁，自创立起专注异型砂轮、带杆磨头等特种磨具生产。

1990s Product Growth产品拓展

Portfolio Expansion品类持续延伸

Polishing wheels, grinding circles, flap wheels, sponge wheels, and wool wheels joined the catalog — serving more industrial finishing applications.

抛光轮、砂圈、页轮、海绵轮、羊毛轮等系列产品陆续投产，覆盖更多工业表面处理场景。

2000s Technology技术引进

Global Process Integration引进国际先进工艺

Advanced technologies from Japan, the United States, and other leading markets integrated into R&D and production — driving continuous product innovation.

引进日本、美国等发达国家先进技术，融入研发与生产体系，持续研制开发新产品。

1,000+ Customers Worldwide服务千家客户

Serving 20+ Export Markets远销二十余国

2026 Four Decades四十年

Continuous Production · Custom at Core持续生产 · 深耕非标

01

Expert Team专业团队

A tiered professional team of senior technical experts and mid-career specialists. Core R&D personnel each bring over 20 years of experience in abrasives formulation and process engineering — providing solid technical support for continuous product innovation.

已形成一支由资深技术专家与中青年骨干构成的阶梯式专业团队，核心研发人员均拥有二十年以上磨具配方研发与工艺实践经验，为持续的产品创新与工艺优化提供了坚实的技术支撑。

02

Production Capacity生产实力

A self-owned manufacturing base in northern Nanchang with full in-house capability from formulation R&D and pressing to high-temperature firing and precision testing — ensuring product quality and delivery stability at the source.

于南昌北郊建成自有产权生产基地，具备从配方研发、压制成型、高温烧成到精密检测的全链条自主生产能力，从源头保障产品品质与交付稳定性。

03

Global Reach市场覆盖

Serving over 1,000 customers with long-term partnerships among hundreds of leading manufacturers worldwide. Products exported to 20+ countries and regions across Europe, the Americas, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East. Custom non-standard products now account for a steadily growing double-digit share of annual revenue.

累计服务客户逾千家，与国内外数百家知名制造企业建立了长期稳定的合作关系，产品远销欧美、东南亚、中东等二十余个国家和地区，在非标特种磨具细分领域持续保持稳固的市场份额。近年来，非标定制类产品营收占比以年均两位数速度稳步递增。

Custom Solutions定制方案

Beyond Products — Custom Grinding Process Solutions不仅是产品，更是磨削工艺的定制方案

Unlike standard off-the-shelf production lines, our core competitive advantage lies in non-standard custom capability. Drawing on four decades of accumulated process databases and extensive field application experience, we precisely match complex operating conditions and have earned a market reputation for "heritage brand, new processes, premium quality."

不同于标准品流水线，我们最核心的竞争力在于非标定制能力。依托四十年积累的工艺数据库与丰富的现场应用经验，我们能够精准匹配各类复杂工况需求，持续收获"老字号、新工艺、高品质"的市场口碑。

Looking ahead, Nanchang Orient Special Abrasives Factory will continue to uphold the spirit of meticulous craftsmanship, deepening our expertise in non-standard special abrasives to deliver more competitive products and solutions for high-end manufacturing clients worldwide. We warmly invite domestic and international partners to visit our Changbei facility and witness four decades of dedicated craftsmanship.

面向未来，南昌东方磨具将继续以精研细磨的工匠精神，深耕非标特种磨具领域，为全球高端制造业客户提供更具竞争力的产品与方案。诚邀海内外客商莅临昌北，共鉴四十载匠心之道。
