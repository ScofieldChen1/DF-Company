Company Profile企业概况

Orient Abrasives东方磨具

Est. 1990 · 36 Years · Special Abrasives Factory · Northern Nanchang

1990 年创立 · 36 年专注 · 特种磨具厂 · 江西南昌北郊

Nanchang Orient Special Abrasives Factory was founded in 1990 in the core of the Chang-Jiu industrial corridor, with direct access to northern Nanchang's transport hub. Over 36 years of dedicated development, we have evolved from an early industry pioneer into a specialized abrasives enterprise focused on precision manufacturing and custom non-standard production. As a source manufacturer with 36 years of industry heritage, we remain committed to delivering the "final millimeter" solutions for precision industrial processing across China and worldwide.

南昌市东方特种磨具厂始创于 1990 年，扎根昌九工业走廊核心区位，坐拥南昌北郊交通枢纽，三十六载深耕不辍，已从改革开放初期的行业拓荒者，成长为集自主研发、精密制造、非标定制于一体的特种磨具专业企业。作为拥有 36 年行业积淀的源头制造厂商，我们始终致力于为中国乃至全球工业精密加工提供"最后一毫米"的解决方案。

We specialize in special abrasives and surface finishing products — polishing wheels, grinding wheels, diamond points, sponge wheels, wool wheels, and related series — serving domestic industry and welcoming overseas customers and export orders.

本厂专注生产抛光轮、砂轮、金刚石磨头、海绵轮、羊毛轮等特种磨具及表面处理产品，服务国内工业客户，同时承接海外订单与出口业务。

With strong technical capabilities and extensive production experience, we support both custom non-standard products and large-scale standard parts manufacturing. All products are produced to national standards, with performance reaching advanced domestic and international levels. Our products reach multiple countries and regions through a mature export network, earning consistent praise from users worldwide. We warmly welcome partners at home and abroad to work with us for mutual success.

本厂技术力量雄厚，生产经营经验丰富，具备非标定制产品与大规模标准件批量生产双重能力。产品严格按国家标准组织生产，各项性能指标达到国内外同类产品先进水平，远销多个国家和地区，出口网络成熟，深受用户好评。热忱欢迎海内外客商携手合作，共创辉煌！

Since 1999, we have been repeatedly recognized as an Advanced Individual Industrial & Commercial Enterprise, regularly honored as an Excellent Taxpayer, and awarded the Best Industrial & Commercial Enterprise title — receiving commemorative trophies from relevant authorities in recognition of our compliant operations, trustworthy tax record, and manufacturing excellence.

自 1999 年起，本厂连年被评为先进个体工商户，常年获授优等纳税大户、最佳工商企业奖等荣誉，并获颁相关奖杯，体现了政府部门与行业对本厂合规经营、诚信纳税与品质制造的持续认可。

Beyond Products — Custom Grinding Process Solutions不仅是产品，更是磨削工艺的定制方案

Unlike standard off-the-shelf production lines, our core competitive advantage lies in non-standard custom capability. 不同于标准品流水线，我们最核心的竞争力在于非标定制能力。依托 36 年积累的工艺数据库与丰富的现场应用经验，我们能够精准匹配各类复杂工况需求，持续收获"老字号、新工艺、高品质"的市场口碑。

Looking ahead, Nanchang Orient Special Abrasives Factory will continue to uphold the spirit of meticulous craftsmanship, deepening our expertise in non-standard special abrasives to deliver more competitive products and solutions for high-end manufacturing clients worldwide. We warmly invite domestic and international partners to visit our Changbei facility and witness 36 years of dedicated craftsmanship.

面向未来，南昌东方磨具将继续以精研细磨的工匠精神，深耕非标特种磨具领域，为全球高端制造业客户提供更具竞争力的产品与方案。诚邀海内外客商莅临昌北，共鉴三十六载匠心之道。
