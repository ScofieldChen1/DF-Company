Partner for Industry 全球工业领域
值得信赖的特种磨具厂

Founded in 1990 — 36 years as a special abrasives factory. Integrated production, QC & logistics, supplying mounted points, polishing wheels and special grinding tools for shipbuilding, casting, aviation, and automotive finishing.

自 1990 年创立，36 年专注特种磨具制造 — 集研发、生产、质检、物流于一体，为造船、铸造、航空、汽车等工业领域提供带柄磨头、抛光系列砂轮及特种磨具。

Key Industries服务行业

Seven Sectors We Serve七大服务行业

Special abrasives tailored to precision grinding, honing, snagging, and finishing across high-end manufacturing.

特种磨具精准匹配磨削、珩磨、荒磨、精加工等高端制造场景。

Automotive汽车制造

Grinding and honing of engine blocks, crankshafts, camshafts, and gears.

发动机缸体、曲轴、凸轮轴、齿轮的磨削和珩磨。

Shipbuilding船舶制造

Snagging and cutting of large castings, forgings, and welded structures.

大型铸锻件、焊接结构件的荒磨和切割。

Aviation航空

Precision grinding of titanium alloy and superalloy blades — high-end abrasives required.

钛合金、高温合金叶片的精密磨削，需高端磨具配套。

Mold & Die模具制造

Cavity and surface finishing, polishing, and deburring for molds and dies.

模具型腔、型面抛光、精加工与去毛刺。

General Machinery通用机械

Finishing of bearings, hydraulic components, and precision parts.

轴承、液压元件及精密零部件的精加工。

Medical Devices医疗器械

Medical tool polishing and needle cutting — PVA sponge wheels commonly applied.

医用刀具抛光、针头切割，常用 PVA 海绵轮。

High-Speed Rail高铁

Rail grinding wheels for track maintenance and finishing.

轨道打磨砂轮，用于铁路轨道维护与精加工。
